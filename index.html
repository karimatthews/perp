Native Ecology and Roadside Diversity Society

Why roadside vegetation matters. It can:

  • Act as a windbreak
  • Provide shelter
  • Reduce fire risk
  • Slow runoff
Roadsides within Victoria contain 25% of all rare or threatened flora species and communities listed under the Flora and Fauna Guarantee Act 1988.

Roadside vegetation acts as a corridor, connecting larger habitat patches.

What can you do?

  1. Maintain fences

    Keep livestock off sensitive roadside vegetation

  2. Control weeds

    Protect vegetation from weed invasion

  3. Leave fallen timber

    Woody debris creates valuable habitat

  4. Encourage spread

    Allow native seedlings to establish in paddocks
